# GSEB Rankers – Class 10 Board Exam Preparation

**Free complete study system for Gujarat Board (GSEB) Class 10 students**

A clean, interactive, and mobile-friendly website designed to help students score **90%+** in the GSEB SSC Board Exams.

---

## Features

- **90-Day Master Plan** – Clear phase-wise preparation strategy
- **High Weightage Chapters** – Subject-wise important chapters with marks
- **Key Formulae** – Important formulae with short explanations
- **Chapter-wise Progress Tracker** – Interactive checklist (progress is saved in browser)
- **Chapter-wise PYQ Guidance** – Focus on high-priority chapters
- **Previous Year Papers** – Direct links to free downloadable papers
- **How to Use Previous Year Papers** – Step-by-step guide
- Fully responsive design
- SEO optimized
- No login required
- Completely free

---

## Subjects Covered

- Mathematics
- Science
- Social Science
- English

---

## How to Use

1. Open the website
2. Follow the **90-Day Plan**
3. Mark chapters as completed in the **Progress Tracker**
4. Practice high-weightage chapters first
5. Download and solve previous year papers regularly
6. Maintain a Mistake Notebook
