Key Formulae

Quadratic Formula
x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a

Used to find roots of quadratic equations. Discriminant decides nature of roots.

Arithmetic Progression
aₙ = a + (n-1)d  |  Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]

nth term and sum of first n terms – highly important.

Ohm’s Law & Mirror Formula
V = IR  |  1/f = 1/v + 1/u

Core formulae for Electricity and Light numericals.

How to Use Previous Year Papers Effectively

  1. Download 5–7 years papers (2019 to latest)
  2. Start with high-weightage chapters only
  3. Solve under exam conditions (3 hours timer)
  4. Check answers and write mistakes in Mistake Notebook
  5. Revise weak areas and re-attempt similar questions
  6. Repeat – Solve at least 8–10 full papers before the exam
Pro Tip: In the last 30 days, solve one full paper every alternate day.
